Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Student Toolkit PH is an offline-first student utility developed and operated by Hiei One Apps. This policy explains what information the app stores, collects, uses, and shares when you use its scheduling, grade calculation, study timer, expense tracking, advertising, analytics, and feedback features.

Information Stored on Your Device

Class schedules, subject and grade records, grading preferences, timer settings, and expense records are stored locally on your device. Student Toolkit PH does not upload this content to our servers. You can delete these records using the available controls in the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Feedback Information

Sending feedback is optional. When you submit the feedback form, we collect your comment and any optional name, grade level, and school that you choose to provide. We also receive the app name, device platform, and submission time. This information is transmitted to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a Google Sheet controlled by Hiei One Apps. We use it only to respond to feedback, investigate problems, and improve the app.

Do not include passwords, financial account details, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive information in feedback.

Analytics

When analytics is enabled in a release, Student Toolkit PH uses PostHog to understand app usage and improve reliability and features. Analytics may include events such as opening a screen or tool, adding or deleting an item, starting or completing a timer, app lifecycle events, and technical information automatically processed by the SDK, such as app version, device or operating-system details, network information, and a pseudonymous identifier.

We do not intentionally include subject names, teacher names, grade values, expense descriptions or amounts, feedback comments, names, grade levels, or school names in analytics events. PostHog is configured with session replay disabled and IP-based geolocation disabled.

Advertising

Student Toolkit PH uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process advertising or device identifiers, IP address, app and device information, diagnostics, and interactions with advertisements to deliver, limit, measure, secure, and report ads. The app requests non-personalized ads and limits ad content to a general-audience rating. Google and participating advertising providers may process this information under their own privacy policies and applicable consent choices.

How We Use and Share Information

We use information only to operate and improve Student Toolkit PH, receive feedback, analyze app usage, display and measure advertisements, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by these service providers only for the functions described above:

  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets for feedback submission and storage
  • PostHog for product analytics
  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and reporting

We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security of the app.

Data Security

Feedback, analytics, and advertising traffic is sent using encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to stored feedback and service dashboards is restricted to authorized accounts. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Submitted feedback is retained while it is useful for support and product improvement, then deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed. Analytics and advertising information is retained according to the configured service settings and the providers' retention policies.

To request access to or deletion of feedback or other information that can reasonably be linked to you, email hiei.one.apps@gmail.com. Include enough detail to identify the submission, but do not send additional sensitive information. We may need to verify the request and may retain information when legally required.

Children's Privacy

Student Toolkit PH is a general student utility and may be used by minors. Users should avoid submitting personal or sensitive information through feedback. A parent or guardian may contact us to request deletion of information submitted by a child. Advertising and analytics settings are configured to reduce data collection and restrict ad content, but parents and guardians should review whether the app is appropriate for the student using it.

International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their handling of information is governed by their terms, privacy policies, and applicable safeguards.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the revised policy with a new effective date before materially different practices are introduced.
